# Musician Practice Alarm

An Android app that reminds you to practice your instrument, and gets more annoying until you do.

- Reminders start at your practice time and repeat, faster each time, for up to 3 hours.
- Honor system first: you can tap "I played" or snooze (each snooze is shorter).
- After you skip too many days, the honor button disappears. The microphone has to hear you play.

## Install it

1. On your Android phone, open the **Releases** page of this repository and tap the newest release.
2. Tap **MusicianPracticeAlarm.apk** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to "install unknown apps", then go back and tap Install.
4. Open Musician Practice Alarm and answer the three setup questions. Allow notifications and the microphone when asked.

## Update it

Download and open the newest MusicianPracticeAlarm.apk. It installs over the old one and keeps your history.

## If reminders don't show up

Some phones put apps to sleep. In the app, tap the buttons under "Can reminders reach you?". On Samsung phones, also remove the app from Settings > Battery > Background usage limits > Sleeping apps.
